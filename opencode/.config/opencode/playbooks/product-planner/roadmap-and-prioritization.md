# Roadmap and Prioritization Playbook

Use this playbook for post-MVP planning and investment sequencing.

## Output contract

1. Candidate improvements (v1.1+)
2. Prioritization scorecard
3. Dependencies and enablers
4. Timeline bands (now/next/later)
5. Decision rationale

## Prioritization dimensions

- Impact (user + business)
- Effort (time/cost/complexity)
- Risk (delivery + product)
- Confidence (quality of evidence)
- Strategic alignment

## Suggested sequencing model

- Now: high impact, low/medium effort, validated assumptions.
- Next: high impact but dependent on enablers.
- Later: speculative bets or high-cost platform changes.

## Anti-pattern checks

- Is this roadmap item outcome-linked?
- Are we over-investing in low-confidence work?
- Are we adding features without telemetry?
- Are we carrying unresolved MVP defects into growth work?
